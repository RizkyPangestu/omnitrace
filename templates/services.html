Frequently asked questions

What is cross-checking?

Comparing information from different sources to identify agreement or inconsistencies. Sources that simply copy one another are not independent evidence.

What if no information is found?

The report explains which sources were checked and the limits of the research. Missing information does not prove that the information does not exist.

What does the results Q&A include?

An explanation of the report within the original scope. Research into new subjects is not included.
